About Me

Engineering light into precision systems

I'm an Electro-Optics Engineer based in Abu Dhabi, UAE, with over 7 years of hands-on experience designing, testing and qualifying opto-electronic and photonic systems for defense and industrial applications.

At Halcon Systems LLC, I work across the full stack of electro-optic development — from PCB schematic design and Interface Control Document (ICD) analysis to fiber-optic assembly, laser testing, and seeker system qualification for guided weapon platforms. I've supported live field trials, calibrated laboratory instrumentation, and delivered technical evaluations that directly informed procurement decisions.

My foundation is in photonics engineering — a 5-year integrated MSc from the International School of Photonics at CUSAT — where I built solid-state lasers and wavelength-division multiplexing systems from first principles. I bring that same rigor to every optical and electronic system I design today.
